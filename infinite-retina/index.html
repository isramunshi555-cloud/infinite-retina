05 / Good questions

A little more clarity.

Do I need support across all seven areas?

No. Start with the area most relevant to your challenge. The framework helps reveal connections; it does not require a full-service engagement.

What does “Seven Voxels” mean?

It is the name of the service framework in the supplied Infinite Retina concept: Funding, Customers, Attention, Infrastructure & Operations, Culture, Talent, and Legal. Each is a distinct perspective on the business.

Can you help with AR and VR experiences?

The Attention service area includes development and production of AR and VR experiences, alongside PR, media, networking, and conference strategy. Specific technical requirements should be discussed when scoping a project.

How are timelines and fees determined?

They depend on the challenge, the services selected, and the agreed deliverables. This concept does not specify fixed pricing or turnaround times.

What should I prepare before a conversation?

A short overview of your business, your most pressing challenge, and the outcome you want. The brief builder below helps you organize those details.

Does the Legal service replace legal counsel?

The supplied concept covers business structure strategy and fairness & equity issues. Regulated legal advice and formal legal work should be scoped with appropriately qualified counsel.
